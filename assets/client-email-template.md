# Client Email Template

Use this as the reference structure for the client-facing proposal email. Students may adapt the wording if the final message stays professional and matches the generated proposal.

**Configurable via Settings (`/settings`), not fixed text:** the greeting line, the sign-off's designation, and the sender company name. Defaults below match the schema defaults; a "Preview email" step also lets the sender add CC/BCC and tweak the subject/body for one specific send before it goes out.

## Subject

Proposal for {{company_name}}

## Body

{{email_salutation_template}} — e.g. "Hi {{client_name}},"

You can view the proposal here: {{proposal_link}}

This document outlines the project scope, timeline, pricing details, and recommended approach.

If you have any questions or would like to make adjustments, feel free to reach out. We are happy to iterate with you.

Looking forward to hearing your thoughts.

Best regards,

{{salesperson_name}}{{, sender_designation if set}}
